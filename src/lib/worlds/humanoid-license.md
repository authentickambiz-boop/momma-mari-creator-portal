# Worlds humanoid license

The humanoid body, clothing shells, skeleton, and animation clips in `src/lib/worlds/humanoid.ts` are original geometry made for this project. `public/worlds/humanoid.glb` is that same rig, exported by `scripts/export-humanoid.ts`.

They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You can copy, change, and use them with no attribution.

No Ready Player Me, MakeHuman, Quaternius, or Kenney mesh is included. Saved character looks stay on version 1 and keep the same hair, clothing, and color ids; the humanoid wears those ids as skinned parts.
